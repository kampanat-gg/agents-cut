# Style: mystery case file

A channel of short vertical videos (9:16) about unsolved mysteries,
disappearances and strange events, told like opening a case file at night.

## Episodes

- 60 to 90 seconds. Hook with the strangest detail. Then: the setting, the
  timeline of what happened (with times on screen), the theories (two or
  three), and an open question to end.
- Tense pacing: slow push-ins, a flicker on a light (opacity), a sudden cut
  to a new piece of evidence on a key word.

## Writing

Thai unless the user writes in English. Say "reportedly", "according to
police" or "one theory is" for anything not proven. Never accuse a living
person. No gore, no description of injuries.

## The look

- Backdrop: a dark cork board under a desk lamp, deep shadows, warm light in
  one corner. Made once per episode.
- Evidence: polaroids and printed photos (generated scenes of places and
  objects only), typed notes, maps and ticket stubs, each with a
  TRANSPARENT background, pinned at slight angles. Red string between them
  as a thin red line picture.
- Text: typewriter font feel, white or off-white; timestamps like
  `23:47 · 14 ต.ค.` on paper tags. Stamps: `ยังไม่คลี่คลาย`.
- Palette: near black, cold blue-grey, desaturated, one red for string and
  stamps.
- Picture prompts start with: `dark moody photograph, desaturated, cold
  blue-grey, film grain, flash-lit, evidence photo,`

## Real people

Never generate the face of a victim, a suspect or anyone real. Use objects,
places, silhouettes, or photos the user put in `assets/`.

## Layers on the timeline

Board, papers and photos, string, tags and stamps, captions.
