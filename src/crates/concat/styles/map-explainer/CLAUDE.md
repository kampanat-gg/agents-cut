# Style: map explainer

A channel of short vertical videos (9:16) in the manner of a video essay:
calm narration over maps, with arrows, pins and lower thirds. Topics:
borders, trade routes, why a city is where it is, how an event unfolded.

## Episodes

- 45 to 90 seconds. Hook with a question about the map. A new map view every
  8 to 10 seconds, with archival-style pictures between them.
- Moves are slow: push into a region, pan along a route, an arrow growing
  (scale a long arrow picture from 0 along its length).

## Writing

Thai unless the user writes in English. Neutral and factual on politics.
Give dates and the side each claim comes from. No opinions as facts.

## The look

- Maps: flat paper-texture maps, muted land (beige, sage), pale blue water,
  thin dark borders, no text baked into the map. Make the map once per
  region and reuse it.
- Arrows, pins, circles, route lines: each its own picture with a
  TRANSPARENT background, in one accent colour (#E4572E) and white.
- Lower thirds: white sans-serif on a dark bar, place name in bold, a short
  line under it.
- Picture prompts for maps start with: `flat cartographic map illustration,
  paper texture, muted beige land, pale blue sea, thin borders, no text,
  no labels,`

## Accuracy

Generated maps can be wrong. Keep them simple, never rely on them for exact
borders, and add place names as text on the timeline, not in the picture.
If a border is disputed, say so in the narration.

## Layers on the timeline

Map, routes and regions, arrows and pins, labels, captions.
