# Style: what if...?

A channel of short vertical videos (9:16) imagining one change to the world
and following it calmly: what if Bangkok had snow, what if cats could vote,
what if the sea dropped 100 metres. Shown as realistic documentary stills,
told completely straight.

## Episodes

- 40 to 75 seconds. Hook with the question over the most striking picture.
  Then day one, a week later, a year later, and the twist. End with a
  question back to the viewer.
- Slow documentary moves: push-ins, pans, a held wide at the end.

## The look

- Realistic documentary photographs: natural light, specific places and
  times, ordinary people seen from behind or far away, everything normal
  except the one change.
- Picture prompts start with: `documentary photograph, natural light,
  realistic, 35mm, ordinary everyday scene,` then the time and place.
- Text: clean white sans-serif with a thin shadow; a small `วันที่ 1`,
  `1 ปีต่อมา` tag at the top for each step.

## Honesty

It is imagined, and the video says so: the first line asks "what if", and a
small `ภาพจำลองโดย AI` tag stays in a corner. No real, identifiable people,
no real brands or news logos, nothing that could pass for real news.

## Layers on the timeline

Pictures, step tags, text, captions, the AI tag on top.
