# Style: vintage engraving 2.5D

A channel of short vertical videos (9:16) about strange, true things: deep sea
animals, old inventions, odd natural laws, forgotten places. Every picture
looks like an engraving or lithograph from a 19th-century natural history
book, cut out and moved in layers, like a paper theatre.

## Episodes

- 40 to 75 seconds. Hook, then 4 to 6 beats, one image per beat, then a
  closing line with a twist or a question.
- Calm, curious narration. Slower than other styles: a move every 3 to 4
  seconds is enough, because the pictures are rich.

## Writing

Thai unless the user writes in English. Short sentences with numbers people
can picture: depths in metres, sizes against a person, years. Say "scientists
think" when it is not settled.

## The look

- Backdrop: aged paper or a hand-tinted engraved landscape, muted indigo,
  sepia and teal, fine cross-hatching. Made once per episode, full frame.
- Subjects: each animal, machine or figure engraved on its own with a
  TRANSPARENT background, so it can move.
- Motion: paper-puppet style. Parts drift, rise and sink, rotate a few
  degrees on a pivot, glow softly (an opacity pulse) for light rays and
  bubbles. No fast zooms.
- Text: small white serif lines in the upper third; a depth or number in a
  larger serif with a thin rule under it.
- Picture prompts start with: `19th-century scientific engraving, fine
  cross-hatching, hand-tinted lithograph, muted indigo and sepia, natural
  history book plate,`

## Layers on the timeline

Backdrop, far elements (light rays, rock, cloud), subjects, near elements
(bubbles, snow, dust), text.

## Sound

A calm voice from a file the user puts in `assets/` (without one, tell it
in short on-screen lines), soft ambient
music. Captions in white on a dark translucent bar.
