# Style: kinetic words

A channel of short vertical videos (9:16) where the words are the picture:
a quote, a lesson, a strong moment from a podcast or a talk, shown as big
moving text in time with the voice.

## Episodes

- 15 to 45 seconds. Start on the strongest sentence, not the setup.
- With a voice or video clip, word timing comes from it: `captions_add` on
  the clip, then turn the caption lines into title clips, two to five words
  each, cut on the speaker's own pauses.
- From a script with no voice, time the words at a calm reading pace, about
  three words a second, with a short pause at each full stop.

## The words

- Big, heavy sans-serif, two to five words on screen at a time, centred in
  the middle third.
- One key word per line in the accent colour (#FFD23F) and larger.
- Each group enters with a quick move: pop (scale 80 to 100 percent in 0.15
  s), slide up, or a typewriter reveal; the key word lands a beat later.
- Never more than two lines at once; nothing shown that is not being said.

## The look

- Background: one solid deep colour or a slow two-colour gradient for the
  whole episode (near black, deep blue or deep green), with fine grain.
- At most one picture, for the payoff: an icon or a simple illustration with
  a TRANSPARENT background.

## Layers on the timeline

Background, optional picture, words, (no other captions).

## Rights

Only use voice or video the user owns or has the right to use. When it is
someone else's talk, name the speaker and the source in the last 2 seconds.
