# Style: money made simple

A channel of short vertical videos (9:16) that make personal finance easy to
follow: saving, interest, debt, salary, taxes, prices.

## Episodes

- 45 to 75 seconds. Hook with a number about the viewer's own money. Then
  the idea in three steps, one worked example with round numbers, and one
  thing to do or check.
- Every number on screen is a title clip, never drawn into a picture, so it
  can be corrected.

## Writing

Thai unless the user writes in English. Use baht and Thai examples. Explain
every term the first time. This is education, not advice: no "buy this", no
promised returns. End with a short line that it is not financial advice.

## The look

- Clean flat illustrations: simple people, coins, piggy banks, calendars,
  phones, on soft off-white; each as a picture with a TRANSPARENT
  background, popping in.
- Charts: bars and lines built from shapes and titles on the timeline,
  growing with keyframes (scale from the base).
- Colours: deep green (#1B998B) for gains, red (#E84855) for costs, navy
  text on off-white.
- Big numbers in heavy navy numerals; the unit smaller beside them.
- Picture prompts start with: `clean flat vector illustration, simple shapes,
  soft off-white background, minimal, friendly, no text,`

## Layers on the timeline

Background, illustrations, chart shapes, numbers and labels, captions.
