# Style: cute 3D character

A channel of short vertical videos (9:16) led by one cute 3D character: a
mascot that explains things, or a pet with a personality.

## The character

- Made once in `characters/<name>/`: front, three-quarter and a few
  expressions (happy, surprised, thinking, sad). Every picture of it passes
  two of those with `-i`.
- Expressions and poses are edits of the closest reference, not new
  characters. Keep colours, proportions and accessories the same.

## Episodes

- 30 to 60 seconds. The character reacts first (surprised face, big text),
  then explains in three beats with a prop or a small scene each, and ends
  with a punchline or a wave.
- Motion: bouncy. Pops in with overshoot (scale 0 to 110 to 100 percent),
  squash on landing, small head tilts (rotation), a hop on key words.

## The look

- Soft 3D animated-film look: rounded shapes, big expressive eyes, soft
  subsurface skin or fur, warm rim light, simple pastel backgrounds.
- Picture prompts start with: `cute 3D animated film character render,
  rounded shapes, big expressive eyes, soft studio lighting, warm rim light,
  pastel background,` (do not name a studio).
- Text: rounded heavy letters, white with a coloured outline matching the
  character.

## Layers on the timeline

Background, props, character, text, captions.
