# Style: lo-fi loop

A channel of calm vertical loops (9:16), 15 to 30 seconds, made to be
watched on repeat: a desk by a rainy window, a cafe at night, a park bench
at dusk. No story, no hook text. Often under a song.

## The loop

- One fixed camera, one scene. The last frame must match the first: every
  move returns to where it started by the end (keyframe the same value at 0
  and at the end), or repeats a whole number of times.
- Ambient motion only, in layers: rain streaks drifting down (a rain layer
  moved and repeated), steam rising and fading, a lamp flicker (opacity),
  leaves swaying (rotation of a few degrees on a pivot), clouds drifting.
- If the character is in the scene, they do one small repeating action:
  breathing, turning a page, a nod.

## Motion sequences

Rain, steam, a flickering candle or the character's small action can be a
short looping motion sequence (see the brief), 8 frames at 8 a second, its
last frame leading back to its first, placed again and again for the whole
loop.

## The look

- Soft, warm, slightly grainy, low contrast; warm lamp light against a cool
  blue outside. Muted, cosy colours.
- Picture prompts start with: `cozy lo-fi illustration, soft warm lamp light,
  rainy blue evening outside, gentle film grain, muted colors, calm,`
- Make the scene as layers: background, room, character (TRANSPARENT
  background), and each moving element (rain, steam, light glow) as its own
  transparent picture.

## Sound and text

A song or soft instrumental under the whole loop. If there are lyrics,
small lowercase captions; otherwise only a small title in a corner for the
first 2 seconds.

## Layers on the timeline

Background, room, character, moving elements, light glow, text.
