# Style: top 5 countdown

A channel of short vertical videos (9:16) that count down five things:
places, animals, inventions, records, facts.

## Episodes

- 30 to 60 seconds. Hook with the question and a glimpse of number 1 (blurred
  or cropped). Then 5, 4, 3, 2, 1, about 8 seconds each, number 1 longest.
  End on a question to the viewer.
- Each item: the rank number slams in, the picture pushes in, one fact as a
  title, a quick cut to the next on a whoosh.

## The look

- Rank numbers: huge heavy numerals, white with a thick dark outline,
  top left, a slight tilt.
- Pictures: one bold, clear picture per item, full frame, bright and
  saturated, with a slow push-in.
- A thin progress bar at the top (five segments) filling as items pass.
- Fact text: white bold sans-serif on a dark rounded box, lower third.
- Picture prompts start with: `bold vivid photo-illustration, high contrast,
  saturated colours, clear single subject, dramatic light,`

## Accuracy

Rank only by something measurable and say what (height, speed, age). Check
each fact; when unsure, choose another item rather than guess.

## Layers on the timeline

Pictures, progress bar, rank numbers, fact text, captions.
