# Style: anime story

A channel of short vertical videos (9:16) told as hand-painted Japanese
anime scenes: everyday moments, small romances, memories, songs. The same
characters in every episode.

## Characters

Characters live in `characters/<name>/`. The first time one appears in this
style, make an anime reference sheet from their photos (front, three-quarter,
side) and save it as `characters/<name>/anime-ref.png`. From then on, pass
`anime-ref.png` and the clearest photo with `-i` for every picture of them.
Keep hair, glasses and build the same in every shot.

## Shots and camera

Plan every episode as a shot list before making pictures. Vary the size and
angle from shot to shot, never two of the same in a row:

- establishing wide: the place, small figure, sky;
- medium: waist up, doing the action;
- close-up: face, one clear emotion, eyes on a specific target;
- insert: hands, an object that matters (a phone, a letter, a cup);
- over the shoulder, toward what they look at;
- low angle for a hopeful or proud beat, high angle for a lonely one.

Keep camera, head and gaze separate: a low camera does not mean looking up.

## Episodes

- 30 to 60 seconds. Hook with an emotional close-up or a striking wide.
  One feeling per episode, landing on the last shot.
- Motion: background layers drift slowly (parallax), hair and petals move
  (small keyframed rotation and position), light flickers through leaves
  (opacity). A cut on every line of narration or lyric.

## The look

- Hand-painted backgrounds, soft golden-hour or blue-hour light, gentle
  bloom, clean cel-shaded characters with crisp line art.
- Layers per scene: sky, far background, near background, character with a
  TRANSPARENT background, foreground (leaves, petals, window frame).
- Text: soft white rounded sans-serif, lower third, small.
- Picture prompts start with: `hand-painted anime film still, cel-shaded
  characters, clean line art, painted background, soft golden light, gentle
  bloom,` (do not name a studio or an artist).

## Layers on the timeline

Sky, background, character, foreground, text, captions.
